Capabilities

What we do

Six kinds of work, one team. Most projects combine two or three: a brand and its website, a store and its migration, an app and the site that introduces it.

WebsitesOnline storesMobile appsBrand identityB2BMigrations and SEO
01

Websites

Marketing and brand sites that load fast, rank well and are easy for your team to update.

  • Strategy, sitemap and page design
  • Custom development with a content editor your team can use
  • Lead forms routed to an inbox or CRM
  • Analytics and search set-up

Related work: NUCAP · KG Marfil Photography

02

Online stores

Custom storefronts for brands that have outgrown a template. Shopify is one of our strongest platforms, and we choose the platform that fits the business.

  • Custom themes and section libraries your team can edit
  • Redesigns and major theme upgrades
  • Product pages, packs, bundles, gifting and upsells
  • Speed and accessibility improvements

Related work: SpiderTech · NRS Brakes · Tummy Tape

03

Mobile apps

iPhone and Android apps built from one codebase, so you launch on both stores without paying for two teams.

  • Sign-in, accounts and cloud sync
  • AI features done responsibly, with privacy built in
  • App Store and Google Play submission, including TestFlight beta
  • Privacy policies and support pages the stores require
04

Brand identity

Logos, colour, type and guidelines that work everywhere, from a 16-pixel favicon to a trade-show banner.

  • Logo design on a construction grid
  • Colour palettes and type systems
  • App icons and social assets
  • Brand guidelines your team and partners can follow
05

B2B and lead generation

For businesses that sell by quote: catalogues organised like a spec sheet, and every inquiry routed to the right person.

  • Structured product data, drawings and part numbers
  • Wishlists and quote requests instead of carts
  • Automated routing with Zapier or your CRM
  • Gated catalogues and downloads

Related work: Modulus

06

Migrations and SEO

Moving off WordPress, Wix or an old theme without losing the search traffic you've earned.

  • SEO audit before anything moves
  • Redirect maps for every page that matters
  • Scripted content and metadata import
  • Post-launch checks

Related work: NUCAP

FAQ

Good questions

Do you only work with Shopify?

No. Shopify is one of our strongest platforms for online stores, but we also build websites, B2B platforms, mobile apps and brand identities.

Can our team update the site after launch?

Yes. We build with editable sections and blocks, and hand over a short guide and a change log.

Can you improve a site we already have?

Yes. Some of our work is new builds, and some is upgrades, new features and fixes on existing stores.

Where are you based?

Toronto, Canada.
