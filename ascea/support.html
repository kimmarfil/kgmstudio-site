Ascea Support

Log what you do in 10 seconds. Find scholarships that fit you. Write essays only you could write.

Need help? Email hello@kgmstudio.ca and we'll reply within 2 business days. Tell us what you were trying to do, and include a screenshot if you can. Please don't send us your sign-in code.

Common questions

How do I sign in?

Enter your email in the Account tab and we'll send you an 8-digit code. There's no password. If the code doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder, then tap to send a new one.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Ascea without signing in, and your stories stay on your phone. Signing in backs them up so you don't lose them and can use them on a new phone, and lets you link a parent.

Why do I need a parent to approve?

If you're 13 or younger, a parent or guardian has to approve before your stories are backed up or the smart features turn on. In the Account tab, tap "Invite a parent" and give them the code. They install Ascea, sign in with their own email, choose "I'm a parent" and enter the code. Until then, you can still log stories on your phone.

How does a parent follow along?

The student makes an invite code in the Account tab (it works for 7 days). The parent installs the app, signs in, chooses "I'm a parent" and enters the code. One parent can follow more than one child: tap "Add another child" on the parent screen.

Can my parents read my stories?

Not unless you allow it. By default, parents see your hours, story titles, deadlines and applications. To let them read the full text, turn on "Let parents read my stories" in the Account tab. You can turn it off, or remove a parent, at any time.

The smart suggestions aren't working

Smart features need an internet connection. If you're 13 or younger, they also need a parent's approval first. You can still type your own title, skills and hours, and the app will try again later.

Are the scholarships real?

Yes. Every scholarship in the app links to its official page. Amounts, deadlines and rules can change, so always check the official page before you apply.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to the Account tab, scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete my account, then type DELETE to confirm. Parents will find it at the bottom of the parent screen. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it. Tip: export your stories first. If you can't open the app, email hello@kgmstudio.ca from the address you sign in with.
