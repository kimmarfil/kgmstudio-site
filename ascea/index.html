AsceaOur own app · Coming to iPhone

Turn what you do into scholarships.

Ascea helps Canadian high-school students log the things they do, match them to real scholarships, and draft applications in their own words. Built by KGM Studio.

See how it works Get launch updates

For students in Grades 9 to 12, anywhere in Canada.

How it works

Three steps from "I did a thing" to an application

01

Tell your story

Tap the + and just say what you did. It takes about 30 seconds. You can also type or add a photo of a certificate. Ascea suggests a title, the skills you showed and the hours it took, and you can change anything.

02

Match real scholarships

178 awards from official pages: 14 open across Canada and 164 by province or territory, picked for your grade and province. Sort by best match, most money per hour of work, or soonest deadline.

03

Draft from your own words

Pick the stories that fit the question and get a first draft built from them. Then edit until it sounds like you, because the best essays come from what you actually did.

Calm by design

Opens to what matters, nothing more

Ascea is built for students opening it for the first time. Home shows your week, one good match and what's coming up. Everything else is a tap away.

  • A weekly recap: stories logged and hours put in.
  • Upcoming deadlines with a simple countdown, so nothing sneaks up.
  • My list keeps what you've saved, started and sent in one place.
  • Light and dark themes, switched in Settings.
For parents

Follow along without hovering

Students can invite a parent to a read-only view: the scholarship money they're a strong fit for, hours logged, applications and upcoming deadlines.

  • Parents see a summary. Full story text only if the student turns on sharing.
  • Students 13 and younger need a parent's approval before stories are backed up or sent to the AI.
  • Parents can't edit anything. It's the student's story bank.
Privacy by design

Built for students, so built carefully

Stored in Canada

Accounts and backups live in Montreal. Sign-in uses one-time email codes, so there's no password to leak.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no analytics trackers, and no access to your location or contacts.

Your voice stays yours

Speech is turned into text on the iPhone wherever it's supported, and Ascea never keeps any audio.

Delete any time

Students and parents can delete their account from inside the app, and everything goes with it.

AI that drafts, not decides

Suggestions and drafts are a starting point. Students edit everything, and nothing is used to train AI models.

Parents in the loop

Under-14 approval follows Quebec's privacy rule, applied across Canada.
