AsceaOur own app · Coming to iPhone

Turn what you do into scholarships.

Ascea helps Canadian high-school students log the things they do, keep their school week on track, match real scholarships, and draft applications in their own words. Built by KGM Studio.

See how it works Get launch updates

For students in Grades 9 to 12, anywhere in Canada, with a read-only view for parents.

How it works

Three steps from "I did a thing" to an application

01

Tell your story

Tap the + and just say what you did. It takes about 30 seconds. You can also type it, or snap a certificate, award or report card. Ascea suggests a title, the skills you showed and the hours it took, and you can change anything.

02

Match real scholarships

More than 180 awards from official pages, across Canada and in every province and territory, picked for your grade and province and ranked by how well your stories fit. Sort by best match, most money per hour of work, or soonest deadline.

03

Draft from your own words

Pick the stories that fit the question and get a first draft built only from what you did, in English or French, short or long. Then edit until it sounds like you.

Planner

Your school week, day by day

Set up your timetable once and Ascea knows what's on today, including Day 1 / Day 2 schedules. Homework, tests and events sit next to your classes, with reminders that arrive when you need them.

  • Timetables that work the way schools do: the same every day, different by weekday, or rotating Day 1 to Day 9, skipping weekends, holidays and PA days.
  • To-dos, tests, events and notes, each linked to a class if you like.
  • Reminders the evening before, the morning of, an hour before or right on time.
  • Study plans: turn a test or project into small daily steps on the days before it's due.
  • A Today card on Home: which day it is, your classes and what's due.
Scholarships

Everything you need to actually apply

Each scholarship page puts the award, the days left and the time it takes to apply up front, then says plainly who can apply and what to send.

  • "Only for" shows awards limited to some students, such as children of a company's employees or union members, so nobody misses one their family qualifies for.
  • A checklist for every application, and a ready-to-send request for a reference letter or nomination.
  • Deadline reminders a week before and the day before.
  • Saved, started and sent applications in one place, with what's ready and what's left.
  • Details are re-checked against the official pages automatically, and new scholarships are added every month after a person reviews them.
Calm by design

Opens to what matters, nothing more

Home shows your day, your week and one good match. Everything else is a tap away.

  • A weekly recap of stories and hours, with a gentle goal.
  • Ontario's 40 community-service hours, tracked as you log them.
  • An activities résumé built from your stories, to share or save as a PDF.
  • An optional reminder to log a story, skipped on days you already did.
  • Light, Dark, or Automatic to follow your phone.
For parents

Follow along without hovering

Parents get their own read-only view: hours logged, applications, upcoming deadlines and the scholarship money their child is a strong fit for.

  • Link up either way: the student shares a code, or the parent sends their child an invite they can accept or decline.
  • The week ahead: if the student shares their planner, parents see homework and tests that are coming up, with a reminder every Sunday. Notes stay private.
  • Story text is shared only if the student turns it on. Parents can't change anything.
  • Students 13 and younger need a parent's approval, confirmed by email, before stories are backed up or sent to the AI.
  • One parent can follow several children and switch between them.

Privacy by design

Built for students, so built carefully

Stored in Canada

Accounts and backups live in Montreal. Sign-in uses one-time email codes, so there's no password to leak. You can also use Ascea without an account, on one phone.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no analytics trackers, and no access to your location, contacts or school email.

Your voice stays yours

Speech is turned into text on the iPhone wherever it's supported, and Ascea never keeps any audio.

Private planner

Your timetable, homework and notes are yours. Parents see them only if you choose to share, and never your notes.

AI that drafts, not decides

Suggestions and drafts are a starting point. Students edit everything, and nothing is used to train AI models.

Delete any time

Students and parents can delete their account from inside the app, and everything goes with it.
