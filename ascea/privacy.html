Ascea Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026 · Updated November 27, 2026 (planner, parent invites, Ascea Plus, parent approval by email)

The short version

Ascea helps high-school students record what they do and find scholarships. We collect only what the app needs to do that. We don't show ads, sell your information, or track you across other apps and websites. If you're 13 or younger, nothing leaves your phone until a parent approves. Your planner is private unless you choose to share it with a parent.

Ascea is made by KGM Studio, a business registered in Ontario, Canada. In this policy, "we" means KGM Studio and "you" means anyone who uses the app: students and parents. If you have questions, email hello@kgmstudio.ca.

Where your information is kept

Everything you enter is saved on your phone first. If you sign in, it's also backed up to our database so you don't lose it and can use it on a new phone. The database is hosted by Supabase in Montreal, Canada.

If you use the app without signing in, your information stays only on your phone.

Your planner is backed up in the same way, only for you. Parents can't see it unless you turn on sharing (see "What parents can see").

Smart features (AI)

Ascea can suggest a title, skills and hours for a story, read a photo, help draft scholarship essays from your stories, and turn a test or project in your planner into a day-by-day study plan. To do this, only the text needed for that request is sent securely to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model. For a story, that's the story text; for a photo, the photo; for a study plan, the item's title, subject, due date and your free days before it. Anthropic processes it in the United States and sends back suggestions.

  • Only what's needed for that one request is sent: never your email address.
  • Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this information to train its AI models. It may keep it for a short time for safety and abuse monitoring, then deletes it.
  • Photos are used only to read them. We don't store the photo on our servers; only the story text you keep is saved.
  • We keep a count of how many smart-feature requests each account makes each month, so we can apply the monthly allowance. The count doesn't include what you wrote.
  • AI suggestions can be wrong. You can always edit them, and essays are drafts for you to rewrite in your own words.

Talking instead of typing (microphone)

When you tap Tap to talk, Ascea uses Apple's built-in speech recognition to turn what you say into text, so your story writes itself. Ascea asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition the first time.

  • The microphone is only on while you're on the talk screen, and you can stop it at any time.
  • On iPhones that support it, speech is recognised on the phone itself. On others, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Ascea doesn't record or keep any audio. Only the text you choose to save becomes part of your story.
  • You can turn the microphone off any time in Settings > Ascea, and type your stories instead.

Reminders

If you turn them on, Ascea reminds you about scholarship deadlines, homework and tests, and to write a story. Parents can turn on a Sunday reminder to look at the week ahead. These reminders are set up and shown by your phone itself: nothing is sent to our servers to deliver them. You can turn them off in the app's Settings, or for Ascea in your phone's Settings.

Students 13 and younger

When you set up the app, you tell us if you're 13 or younger. If you are, your stories and planner stay only on your phone, and the smart features stay off, until a parent or guardian links to your account and approves. We do this because Quebec's privacy law requires a parent's consent for children under 14, and we apply the same rule across Canada.

A parent approves from the parent side of the app after linking up, either with a code from the student or by sending the student a code of their own, which the student must accept. To make sure it's really a parent, the approval is confirmed by email: we send a one-time link to the email address the parent signed in with, and approval only counts once it's used. We then send a second email confirming it, with a link to withdraw approval at any time. Parents can also contact us to withdraw consent.

What parents can see

A student and a parent can link up in two ways: the student gives the parent a code, or the parent sends the student a code from the parent side of the app. When a parent sends a code, nothing is shared until the student taps Allow.

Linked parents get a read-only view: hours, story titles, skills, saved scholarships, deadlines and application progress. Parents can't change anything.

Two things stay private unless the student chooses otherwise, in Account → Parent access:

  • Story text: parents see the full text of stories only if the student turns on "Let parents read my stories".
  • The planner: parents see homework, tests and events with their dates only if the student turns on "Share my planner with parents". Planner notes and the class timetable are never shared.

Students can remove a linked parent at any time, and parents can stop following at any time.

Ascea Plus

Ascea Plus is an optional subscription bought through the App Store, by a student or by a parent for up to three linked children.

  • Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We use RevenueCat to check whether an account has Plus. It receives a random account ID (not your name or email address) and the purchase record from Apple.
  • A student covered by a parent's plan sees which parent covers them. If the parent and student unlink, that child is no longer covered.
  • "Ask a parent" in the app opens your phone's share sheet with a message you send yourself. We don't send it or see who it goes to.

To cancel, go to your Apple ID's subscriptions in your phone's Settings. Deleting your Ascea account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, stories, planner, scholarship activity, parent links, Plus records and smart-feature usage counts are deleted from our database right away. Copies in our encrypted backups are removed within 30 days as those backups expire. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

Signing out also erases everything Ascea saved on that phone.

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your information: most of it you can view and edit in the app. For a full copy, email us.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account → Danger zone → Delete my account (parents: at the bottom of the parent screen). If you can't open the app, email hello@kgmstudio.ca from the address you sign in with and we'll do it for you.
  • Control parent access: in the Account tab, invite or remove parents, accept or decline a parent's code, and choose whether they can read your stories and see your planner.
  • Withdraw consent: you (or, for students 13 and younger, a parent) can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the account.

If you're not satisfied with how we handle a privacy request, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Security

Information is encrypted when it travels between your phone and our servers. Our database only lets each account read its own information, and linked parents only see the summary described above. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach puts you at risk.

Changes to this policy

If we make an important change, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in the app before it takes effect.
