Ascea Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

Ascea helps high-school students record what they do and find scholarships. We collect only what the app needs to do that. We don't show ads, sell your information, or track you across other apps and websites. If you're 13 or younger, nothing leaves your phone until a parent approves.

Ascea is made by KGM Studio, a business registered in Ontario, Canada. In this policy, "we" means KGM Studio and "you" means anyone who uses the app: students and parents. If you have questions, email hello@kgmstudio.ca.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code. We never ask for a password.
Profile: first name, grade, province, interests, whether you're 13 or younger, and whether you're a student or a parentTo match you with scholarships, show your roadmap, and know when a parent needs to approve.
Your stories: what you write or say, plus the title, skills, hours and dateThis is the heart of the app: so you can find your stories later and use them in applications and essays.
Scholarship activity: scholarships you save and where your applications standTo remind you of deadlines and track your progress.
Parent links: the parent's first name, and which student and parent accounts are linkedSo a parent you invite can follow along.
Photos you choose (for example, a certificate or report card)To read the details off the photo and turn them into a story. We don't keep the photo itself; see "Smart features" below.

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, or any advertising identifier, and we don't use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Where your information is kept

Everything you enter is saved on your phone first. If you sign in, it's also backed up to our database so you don't lose it and can use it on a new phone. The database is hosted by Supabase in Montreal, Canada.

If you use the app without signing in, your information stays only on your phone.

Smart features (AI)

Ascea can suggest a title, skills and hours for a story, read a photo, and help draft scholarship essays from your stories. To do this, the text of the story (or the photo) is sent securely to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model. Anthropic processes it in the United States and sends back suggestions.

  • Only what's needed for that one request is sent: never your email address.
  • Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this information to train its AI models. It may keep it for a short time for safety and abuse monitoring, then deletes it.
  • Photos are used only to read them. We don't store the photo on our servers; only the story text you keep is saved.
  • AI suggestions can be wrong. You can always edit them, and essays are drafts for you to rewrite in your own words.

Students 13 and younger

When you set up the app, you tell us if you're 13 or younger. If you are, your stories stay only on your phone, and the smart features stay off, until a parent or guardian links to your account and approves. We do this because Quebec's privacy law requires a parent's consent for children under 14, and we apply the same rule across Canada.

A parent can approve from the parent side of the app after linking with an invite code from the student. Parents can contact us to withdraw consent at any time.

What parents can see

A student can invite a parent with a 6-character code. Linked parents get a read-only view: hours, story titles, skills, saved scholarships, deadlines and application progress. Parents can't change anything.

Parents see the full text of stories only if the student turns on "Let parents read my stories" in the Account tab. Students can remove a linked parent at any time, and parents can stop following at any time.

Who else handles your information

We use a few service providers to run the app. Each one only gets what it needs:

Information handled in the United States is subject to US law. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We would only disclose it if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, stories, scholarship activity and parent links are deleted from our database right away. Copies in our encrypted backups are removed within 30 days as those backups expire.

Signing out also erases everything Ascea saved on that phone.

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your information: most of it you can view and edit in the app. For a full copy, email us.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account → Danger zone → Delete my account (parents: at the bottom of the parent screen). If you can't open the app, email hello@kgmstudio.ca from the address you sign in with and we'll do it for you.
  • Control parent access: invite or remove parents, and choose whether they can read your stories, in the Account tab.
  • Withdraw consent: you (or, for students 13 and younger, a parent) can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the account.

If you're not satisfied with how we handle a privacy request, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Security

Information is encrypted when it travels between your phone and our servers. Our database only lets each account read its own information, and linked parents only see the summary described above. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach puts you at risk.

Changes to this policy

If we make an important change, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in the app before it takes effect.
