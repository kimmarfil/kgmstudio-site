Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: we only collect what you choose to send us, we use it to reply to you, and we never sell it.

Last updated November 26, 2026

Who we are

KGM Studio is a web and app studio registered in Ontario and based in Toronto, Canada. This policy covers the website at kgmstudio.ca. Our app, Ascea, has its own privacy policy.

If you have a question about your information, email us at hello@kgmstudio.ca.

What we collect

When you send us a message

Our contact form asks for your name, your email address, your company or brand (optional), what your message is about, and the message itself. We receive the same kind of information if you email us directly.

When you browse the site

We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and we don't create a profile of you. Like any website, the services that deliver our pages see technical details such as your IP address and browser; those are listed below.

How we use it

  • To read and reply to your message, and to keep a record of our conversation.
  • If we end up working together, to plan and run the project.

We don't add you to a mailing list, we don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services needed to run this website and our email.

Services we use

  • FormSubmit passes contact-form messages to our inbox. It handles the message only to deliver it, and may process it outside Canada.
  • Microsoft 365 hosts our email at hello@kgmstudio.ca, where messages are stored.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log technical details such as IP addresses to keep the service running and secure.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces this site uses, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads. Google states that it does not use this to profile visitors.

Some of these providers store or process information outside Canada, where it is protected by their own policies and may be subject to local laws.

How long we keep it

We keep messages only as long as we need them to reply, to look after a project if we work together, and to meet our business record-keeping duties. After that, we delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@kgmstudio.ca and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Keeping it safe

This site is served over a secure connection (HTTPS), and we limit access to messages to the people at KGM Studio who need them to reply or to work with you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
