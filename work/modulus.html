An inquiry-first B2B catalogue for precision tape

B2B ecommerceUX/UI designWeb developmentAutomationShopify
Visit modulustape.com ↗

Modulus makes private-label and pre-cut kinesiology tape shapes for clinics, brands and distributors. Their buyers don't add things to a cart: they compare shapes and specs, then ask for a quote. Our job was to build a storefront that works the way B2B buying actually happens, and hands every serious inquiry straight to the sales team.

01 · Catalogue

A catalogue built for people who read spec sheets

Buyers arrive knowing the body region, width and adhesive they need. We turned every shape into structured data, so the catalogue can be filtered like a parts database and each product page shows the drawing and part number a buyer needs to sign off internally.

  • A horizontal filter bar for availability, category, shape and edge finish
  • Technical drawings and part numbers stored as product data, not pasted images
  • Accessible tabbed specifications that work with a keyboard and screen readers
  • Prices hidden across the store, so every quote starts a conversation
02 · Inquiries

From wishlist to sales follow-up, automatically

Instead of a cart, buyers build a wishlist of shapes and send one inquiry for all of them. Behind the scenes, each request is routed to the right people and the buyer gets an immediate, useful reply.

  • Wishlist drawer that collects shapes across the whole catalogue
  • Pop-up inquiry and contact forms with confirmation emails to the buyer
  • Four automated routing paths through Zapier, each sending requests to the right place
  • Catalogue requests email the PDF instantly and add opted-in buyers to the marketing list
03 · Section library

Fifty sections the team can run without us

The Modulus team updates certifications, markets and product copy often. We built a library of custom sections on Shopify's Horizon theme, then reorganized every section's settings the same way, so anyone on the team can build a new page in the editor.

  • Hero, specs, process, certifications, FAQ, testimonials and product sliders
  • A stats section with an optional "Markets we serve" icon grid
  • Consistent settings across all sections, documented in a change log
  • Mobile spacing tuned section by section, not with one global setting
