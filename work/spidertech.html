Packs, bundles and a cleaner cart

Visit spidertech.com ↗

SpiderTech sells clinically tested pre-cut kinesiology tape to athletes, patients and clinics, in single pieces, packs and bundles. Their store ran on an older, heavily customized version of its theme. We upgraded it without losing that work, then rebuilt the features that sell multi-packs and bundles and fixed the rough edges in the cart.

01 · Upgrade

A major theme upgrade that kept every customization

Custom work lived inside an old version of the store's theme, which made updates risky. We moved the store to the current major version and carried every customization across, so the team got the new theme's improvements without losing what made their store theirs.

  • Upgraded from version 4 to version 5 of the theme
  • Pack and bundle pricing logic carried over
  • 52 product templates moved across intact
  • Product gallery behaviour and analytics preserved
02 · Packs and bundles

Making the better deal the obvious choice

Multi-packs and bundles are better value for customers and better business for SpiderTech. We made them easier to see and choose on every product page.

  • A pack picker with "Recommended" and "Best value" ribbons
  • A savings bubble and custom review stars in the price block
  • Product bundles that show exactly what's included
  • Eight older content snippets merged into two cleaner ones
03 · Cart and content

A cart that helps instead of confusing

Small cart problems cost sales quietly. We fixed the ones we found, added a well-timed upsell, and refreshed the content that helps people choose.

  • Fixed discount codes that triggered a false "invalid code" message at checkout
  • A bundle upsell and free-item offer in the cart drawer
  • A P.O. box notice before checkout, so shipping surprises don't happen later
  • A compiled reviews section, a modern video player and a "Find your tape" shortcut in the mobile menu
Results

Upgraded, without starting over

SpiderTech runs on the current version of its theme with every customization intact, and the store now guides customers toward packs and bundles from product page to cart.
