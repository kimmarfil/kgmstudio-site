Our own app, from idea to launch

Product designWeb appiPhone appAIBackendSubscriptions
Visit ascea.ca ↗

High-school students do a lot that could win them scholarships, but it goes unrecorded, and the applications ask for essays they don't know how to start. Ascea lets them log what they do in a few seconds, matches them to real Canadian scholarships and drafts applications from their own stories. We designed it, built it and run it.

01 · Stories

Say what you did, get a story back

Logging has to take less effort than forgetting. Students tap to talk or type a line, and Ascea's AI turns it into a titled story with skills and hours they can edit.

  • Speech to text on the phone, a photo of a certificate, or a quick line on the computer
  • Claude suggests the title, skills and hours, with monthly limits so costs stay predictable
  • Ontario's 40 community hours tracked as students go
02 · Scholarships

Real awards, kept up to date

Scholarship lists go stale fast. Ascea's list lives in the cloud and checks itself, so students see deadlines and amounts they can trust.

  • 178 scholarships from official pages, matched to each student's grade, province and stories
  • A scheduled job re-reads each official page and only updates a deadline or amount when it can quote the change
  • New awards found each month, one region at a time, and published only after a review
03 · Essays

First drafts in the student's own words

The hardest part of applying is the blank page. Ascea picks the stories that fit the question and writes a first draft from them, which the student then makes their own.

  • Drafts built only from what the student logged, never invented achievements
  • Make it shorter, Sound more like me, and Undo
  • A checklist per scholarship, reminders before deadlines, and a request for a reference letter
04 · Planner and parents

A reason to open it every day

Scholarships are seasonal, homework isn't. A school planner keeps students coming back, and parents get a calm read-only view instead of nagging.

  • Timetables for any school: the same every day, by weekday, or rotating Day 1, Day 2 cycles
  • Parent accounts linked by code, with a family dashboard, notes and a Sunday week-ahead email
  • Parent approval by email for students under 14, data stored in Canada, no ads or tracking
Results

One app, every screen

One codebase runs the iPhone app and the web app, on the same accounts and data. Built with Expo and React Native, Supabase in Canada, Claude for the AI and RevenueCat for subscriptions.
