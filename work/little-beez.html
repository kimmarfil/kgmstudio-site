Home/Work/Little Beez

A warmer, easier shop for little eaters

Redesign conceptUX/UI designEcommerceTheme developmentShopify

Little Beez sells baby bibs and feeding essentials. We liked the brand and saw room to make the store easier to shop, better at selling gifts and smarter at growing each order from the cart, so we built a redesign concept as a real, working theme on the same version their store already runs. It's a pitch, not a commissioned project, and it shows how we approach a redesign: start from what shoppers need, then build it properly.

01 · Shopping

Find the right bib in two clicks

Parents shop by age, by occasion and by budget. The concept's navigation and collection pages are built around those three questions.

  • Category navigation in the header, with a slide-in shop menu on phones
  • Promo tiles placed inside the product grid, not just above it
  • Best-seller badges driven by product tags the team already uses
  • A compare section and gift-ready sets for quick decisions
02 · Gifting

Product pages that sell the gift

A lot of baby products are bought for someone else. The product page makes that easy without getting in the way of parents buying for themselves.

  • Gift messages that can be sent now, on delivery or on a chosen date
  • A short highlights checklist under the price
  • Reviews and a lifestyle gallery further down the page
  • Sets that bundle the bibs people buy together
03 · The cart

A cart that grows the order

The biggest idea in the concept. Most of Little Beez's products cost less than the free-shipping threshold, so the cart does the selling: it shows how close the order is to free shipping and puts the right add-ons one tap away, without ever leaving the cart.

Cart drawer · desktop

Free-shipping progress

A live bar that counts down to free shipping ("Spend $3.99 more") and celebrates when the order gets there.

"Grab one more" panel

On desktop a second panel slides out beside the cart with small add-ons like plates, bowls and spoons. On phones it becomes a swipeable row.

Featured add-on

One hero product with its colour picker right in the cart, so adding it takes a single tap.

Smart suggestions

Anything already in the cart drops out of the suggestions, so shoppers never see what they've just added.

Gift message, note and coupon

Three tidy buttons for the extras. Gift messages can be sent when the order is placed, when it's delivered, or on a date the buyer picks.

Express checkout30-day returnsSecure checkout
04 · Brand moments

Small touches that feel like the brand

The details carry the warmth: messages that rotate along the top, a "why Little Beez" band, real posts from their community and a footer that invites people to "join the hive".

  • A rotating announcement bar for shipping and gift offers
  • An Instagram grid that always shows their six latest posts
  • A four-column footer with a newsletter sign-up and social links
  • Collection and page heroes in the brand's cocoa and butter palette
Outcome

A concept they could install tomorrow

Because it's built on the same theme version as their live store, the concept isn't a mock-up: it's a working theme that could be previewed on their own products.
