KG Marfil Photography is a Toronto wedding photographer covering weddings, engagements, small and city hall weddings, portraits and films across the GTA. It's also our founder's own studio, which made it the place to build everything a modern photography business needs: a site that sells the work, helps couples plan and book, and then delivers their photos privately after the day.

01 · The work first

An editorial site that lets the photographs lead

The site started as a static design and was rebuilt as a custom theme, then refreshed in October 2026 with a black-and-white brand: square corners, a serif for headlines, a light sans for reading, and photographs given the whole screen.

  • About 30 custom sections across 35 page templates
  • An Instagram-style portfolio viewer that feels natural on a phone
  • Films, reviews, FAQ, venues and a journal, all editable in the theme editor
  • Self-hosted brand fonts, preloaded so headlines appear without a flash
02 · Plan and book

From "how much?" to a real inquiry

Couples want a price before they write. The site gives them one, then turns that interest into an inquiry with everything the photographer needs to reply.

  • A price estimator that carries the couple's choices into the inquiry form
  • Pricing that switches to next year's rates automatically on January 1
  • A "check your date" bar and Messenger button on phones
  • Analytics that follow the whole funnel: form started, abandoned and sent, pricing views and contact taps
03 · After the wedding

Private galleries and wedding websites, built in

Most photographers pay for separate gallery and wedding-site services. These are built into the studio's own site, so couples stay in one place from inquiry to final download.

  • Password-protected client galleries with chapters, favourites and a masonry grid
  • Photos served from Cloudflare in three sizes, so galleries load fast on any connection
  • "Download all" zips built from the originals, never re-compressed
  • Wedding websites couples edit themselves, with three layouts and RSVPs
