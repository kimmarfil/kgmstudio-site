Custom features that sell more brakes

NRS makes galvanized brake pads and rotors for drivers in Canada and the USA. Their store already ran on a premium theme, so the job wasn't a rebuild: it was a set of focused features, each solving a specific problem in how people find parts and fill their cart.

01 · Vehicle finder

A vehicle finder that never leaves a driver stuck

Most visitors start by searching for their vehicle. The finder relies on a third-party app that could occasionally hang on its loading spinner and leave a shopper stuck. We wrapped it in our own section with a safety net.

  • A custom finder section with clear headings on every page it appears
  • If the app errors or takes longer than 10 seconds, shoppers get part-number search and fitment help instead
  • A search icon added to the header and a search button on mobile
02 · Rotor upsell

The right rotors, offered at the right moment

Drivers replacing pads often need rotors too. When a pad set goes into the cart, a pop-up now offers the exact rotors that fit, with their specs, so adding them takes one tap.

  • Pad-to-rotor matches generated from the catalogue data, with an exclusions list from the catalogue team
  • Rotor specs shown in the pop-up, so shoppers know what they're adding
  • Upsold items tagged in each order, and a report that tracks upsell sales against a baseline
  • Live in the store since November 2026
03 · Cleanup

Two page-builder apps, replaced with native sections

Parts of the store were built with two drag-and-drop page-builder apps, which added extra scripts and moving parts. We rebuilt those pieces as native theme sections, then removed the apps and everything they left behind.

  • A features comparison section and a logo heading section rebuilt natively
  • Both apps uninstalled and 42 leftover files removed from the theme
  • 117 live pages checked afterwards to confirm nothing broke
  • A theme error in an existing brand-images section fixed along the way
Results

A store that sells and stays fast

Drivers always have a way to find their part, the cart suggests the rotors they likely need, and the theme carries less third-party weight.
